## Case Study 1: Sipchem - Enterprise-Wide Cultural & Operational Transformation

**Client:** Sipchem (Saudi Arabia's largest private petrochemical company)

**Challenge:**
Sipchem sought a 360° cultural transformation to embed process and occupational safety across nine plants. The company aimed to shift mindsets and governance practices from the CEO to frontline supervisors while delivering measurable operational and financial gains.

**Approach & Solution:**

1. **Cultural embedding:** Coached senior leadership and frontline supervisors to drive safety ownership at every level.

2. **Communication cascade:** Implemented level-appropriate communication tools to ensure consistent messaging and engagement.

3. **Introduced Plant-wide integration:** Rolled out an integrated dashboard across all digital displays, linking safety and operational performance.

4. **Stakeholder buy-in:** Secured full alignment from executives through to frontline teams to sustain momentum beyond the project window.

**Impact:**
- Four consecutive years with zero fatalities and zero Tier 1 process safety incidents.
- SAR 100 million in additional annual EBITDA within the first year, equating to a 3% EBITDA margin gain.
- Embedded a lasting safety culture and governance model, ensuring sustainable impact well beyond program completion.

## Case Study 2: Sipchem - Process Safety Governance & KPI Framework Redesign

**Client:** Sipchem (Saudi Arabia's largest private petrochemical company)

**Challenge:**
Sipchem needed to re-engineer its process safety governance to improve accountability and visibility across nine plants. Safety KPIs were inconsistently collected, reported, and reviewed, leaving executives without a clear line of sight from operations to board-level oversight.

**Approach & Solution:**

1. **Redesigned KPI system:** Standardized the collection, reporting, and review of process safety indicators across the enterprise.

2. **Integrated dashboard:** Developed a consolidated dashboard, leveraged monthly by the VP of Operations and all General Managers, to review and act on performance.

3. **Governance cadence:** Introduced structured meeting agendas, decision logs, and disciplined cadences for executive and operational reviews.

4. **Cross-functional alignment:** Oversaw 14 critical workstreams—including Mechanical Integrity and Quality Assurance—to ensure data quality and alignment with corporate priorities.

**Impact:**
- Created a single source of truth for process safety performance.
- Enhanced executive and board confidence through evidence-based reporting and faster decision-making.
- Institutionalized a governance rhythm that improved accountability, reduced risk, and enabled proactive capital allocation.

## Case Study 3: QatarGas - Comprehensive Process Safety Assessment

**Client:** QatarGas

**Challenge:**
QatarGas needed a full-scale assessment of process safety practices across its main facility. The company required an objective review of its systems, standards, and operations to identify strengths, gaps, and opportunities for improvement—ultimately to shape a multi-year safety improvement program.

**Approach & Solution:**

1. **Assessment leadership:** Directed a multidisciplinary team of international process safety experts on-site.

2. **Scope:** Conducted a comprehensive review covering 14 critical process safety areas, including mechanical integrity, operations, training, and governance.

3. **Stakeholder engagement:** Worked closely with facility leadership and operational teams to ensure findings were actionable and aligned with organizational priorities.

4. **Clarity and prioritization:** Delivered a structured view of both strengths and vulnerabilities, enabling leadership to prioritize the highest-impact improvements.

**Impact:**
- Provided the foundation for a multi-year process safety improvement program, later adopted by QatarGas.
- Enabled executives to make informed decisions about investment and operational priorities.
- Built a clear, evidence-based roadmap for raising safety performance in alignment with global best practices.

## Case Study 4: Kuwait Oil Company (KOC) - Production Optimization

**Client:** Kuwait Oil Company (KOC)

**Challenge:**
KOC faced consecutive years of declining production. Leadership mandated a rapid production increase within six months—without new capital expenditure—while ensuring long-term priorities were not compromised.

**Approach & Solution:**

1. Conducted a comprehensive review of planning, drilling, completion, and reservoir functions.

2. Established a cross-functional "war room" with planning, drilling, and reservoir optimization leaders to accelerate decision-making.

3. **Revamped the planning process** from a linear, waterfall model to a parallelized approach, cutting cycle time nearly in half.

4. Implemented process improvements, digital tools, and optimized coordination across business units.

5. Provided strategic prioritization of workovers over new or exploratory drilling to maximize returns without CAPEX.

**Impact:**
- Delivered a sustained multi-percentage-point production increase within six months.
- Achieved results with zero additional capital expenditure.
- Reduced planning cycle time from 18 months to 8 months, ensuring faster responsiveness and alignment with corporate objectives.
- Strengthened governance and cross-unit collaboration, creating a durable framework for future production planning.

## Case Study 5: Sudapet - Production Optimization

**Client:** Sudapet

**Challenge:**
After several years of declining production across its fields, Sudapet faced urgent pressure to reverse the trend. The organization lacked cohesive governance and struggled to align internal stakeholders around short-term production gains and longer-term investment.

**Approach & Solution:**

1. Established an enterprise-wide governance framework that provided the CEO and board with full visibility from headquarters to the wellhead.

2. Implemented integrated reporting pipelines and developed a real-time CEO dashboard for decision-making.

3. Standardized a single reporting framework to satisfy both internal and external stakeholders, enabling faster capital injection approvals.

4. Strengthened executive governance through concise agendas, decision logs, and targeted leadership coaching.

5. Conducted baseline mapping and prioritized fast infills and workovers to deliver quick production uplift without large capital commitments.

**Impact:**
- Achieved the first production increase in five years, reversing decline and stabilizing cash flow.
- The board gained unprecedented clarity on loss drivers and investment priorities, enabling faster and evidence-based capital approvals.
- Institutionalized a new governance model: shorter meetings, clearer accountabilities, and a unified performance view across the enterprise.
