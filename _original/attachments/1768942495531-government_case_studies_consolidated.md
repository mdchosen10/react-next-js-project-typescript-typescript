# Case Study: Saudi Royal Court PMO - Strategic Development for Three Mega-Cities

**Industry:** Government / National Development  
**Client Type:** Saudi Royal Court

## CHALLENGE

The Saudi Royal Court was in the planning phases of three transformative mega-city projects including NEOM and the Red Sea Development—multi-hundred-billion-dollar initiatives designed to diversify Saudi Arabia's economy and establish world-class urban centers. The challenge was unprecedented:

- Coordinating strategy development across three parallel giga-projects
- Managing 9 different strategy consulting firms (McKinsey, BCG, Bain, Strategy&, etc.)
- No unified operating model to ensure alignment across workstreams
- Complex stakeholder landscape including Royal Court, multiple ministries, and international partners
- Need for integrated planning frameworks spanning tourism, real estate development, economic forecasting, and infrastructure

**The execution infrastructure problem:** Despite having access to world-class strategy consulting firms, there was no central execution mechanism to coordinate across firms, consolidate deliverables, or ensure alignment with senior decision-makers' priorities.

## OUR APPROACH

**Served as Chief of Staff Equivalent for Royal Court PMO**

### 1. Established Operating Model for Multi-Firm Coordination (Months 1-3)

- Designed and implemented visual management boards setting tasks, responsibilities, and objectives for each workstream
- Created governance structure with bi-weekly executive reviews with senior Royal Court leadership
- Established clear escalation paths and decision-making protocols
- Implemented stakeholder management system coordinating C-suite executives and ministerial leadership across multiple initiatives

### 2. Cross-Functional Workstream Management (Ongoing)

- Developed various workstreams to oversee strategy development for each project area:
  - Tourism strategy and infrastructure
  - Real estate development frameworks
  - Economic forecasting and financial modeling
  - Infrastructure planning and coordination
  
- Each team broke into sub-teams working on respective areas
- Teams provided consolidated reports for assessment and feedback
- Responsible for coalescing finished products for senior-most decision-makers in the Kingdom

### 3. Extensive Stakeholder Engagement & Alignment

- Conducted extensive stakeholder meetings to secure buying from executives through frontline teams
- Facilitated coordination between 9 strategy consulting firms to ensure coherent, non-conflicting recommendations
- Managed expectations and ensured momentum beyond individual project windows
- Translated high-level strategic vision into actionable implementation roadmaps

### 4. Integrated Planning & Reporting Framework

- Established annual planning frameworks connecting strategic vision to execution milestones
- Created reporting structure enabling Royal Court to track progress across all three mega-cities simultaneously
- Implemented workshop facilitation and process optimization reducing planning friction

## VALUE REALIZED

### Organizational Impact

- **First unified execution framework** for managing multiple strategy consulting firms simultaneously
- **Clear accountability structure** where previously there was fragmentation across consulting teams
- **Accelerated decision-making** through structured governance rhythms and consolidated reporting
- **Strategic alignment** across three parallel giga-projects with hundreds of billions in planned investment

### Execution Excellence

- Successfully coordinated strategy development across tourism, real estate, economic forecasting, and infrastructure domains
- Provided senior decision-makers in the Kingdom with consolidated, actionable recommendations
- Established sustainable operating model that continued beyond consulting engagement

### Timeline

- 3 years of continuous PMO leadership (October 2014 - May 2017)
- Managed 7-11 direct reports at any given time
- Coordinated workstreams across 9 strategy consulting firms

## KEY SUCCESS FACTORS

**"What made this different than typical strategy consulting?"**

Unlike traditional consulting engagements that deliver recommendations and leave:

- Built the execution infrastructure to coordinate multiple strategy firms simultaneously
- Stayed embedded as Chief of Staff equivalent ensuring continuous alignment
- Established governance rhythms connecting strategic planning to implementation
- Created accountability mechanisms for both consulting firms and government stakeholders
- Translated complex strategy work into actionable roadmaps for decision-makers

**Strategic Impact:**

This work contributed to the successful launch and continued development of NEOM, Red Sea Development, and other transformative Saudi mega-projects that are reshaping the Kingdom's economic future.

### Client Impact

The operating model established during this engagement became the template for how Saudi Arabia coordinates large-scale, multi-stakeholder national transformation initiatives—demonstrating that execution infrastructure is as critical as strategic vision for nation-building projects.

# Case Study: Ministry of Interior - Integrated National Security Intelligence Platform

**Industry:** Government / National Security  
**Client Type:** National Government Agency

## CHALLENGE

Saudi Arabia's Ministry of Interior faced a critical intelligence gap: national security operations were fragmented across multiple directorates with no unified visibility. Decision-makers lacked real-time insights spanning:

- Border security and customs enforcement
- Critical infrastructure protection
- Interior security operations
- Cross-directorate coordination and threat assessment

**The execution infrastructure problem:** Security data resided in siloed legacy systems across dozens of agencies. No integrated analytics platform existed to provide leadership with actionable intelligence for national security decision-making.

## OUR APPROACH

### 1. Intelligence Requirements Definition (Weeks 1-4)

- Conducted classified stakeholder workshops across all security directorates
- Mapped data sources across border control, infrastructure security, and interior operations
- Identified 100+ critical KPIs spanning threat indicators, operational readiness, and response times
- Established data governance framework for sensitive national security information

### 2. Integrated BI Platform Development (Months 2-6)

- Built comprehensive BI platform integrating data from all national security directorates
- Designed role-based access controls ensuring appropriate visibility by security clearance level
- Created unified data model connecting previously siloed intelligence sources
- Implemented automated alerting for critical threat indicators

### 3. Executive Intelligence Dashboards (Ongoing)

- Developed Tableau-based executive dashboards for ministerial leadership
- Created operational views for directorate commanders
- Built real-time monitoring capabilities for security operations centers
- Established daily intelligence briefing protocols leveraging platform insights

## VALUE REALIZED

### Strategic Impact

- **First unified view** of national security posture across all directorates
- **Real-time threat visibility** replacing fragmented, manually-compiled reports
- **Enhanced coordination** between border security, infrastructure protection, and interior operations
- **Data-driven resource allocation** based on integrated threat intelligence

### Operational Excellence

- 100+ KPIs tracked in real-time across national security operations
- Reduced intelligence reporting cycle from days to hours
- Enabled proactive threat response through automated alerting
- Created foundation for AI-powered predictive security analytics

### Timeline

- 6 months from requirements to production deployment
- Platform continues to serve as cornerstone of national security intelligence

## KEY SUCCESS FACTORS

Unlike typical government IT projects that deliver static reports:

- Built production-grade intelligence platform integrated with classified systems
- Established data governance enabling secure cross-directorate information sharing
- Created sustainable operating model for continuous intelligence enhancement
- Positioned platform as foundation for future AI/ML threat prediction capabilities

**National Security Impact:**

This platform transformed Saudi Arabia's approach to national security intelligence—shifting from reactive, fragmented reporting to proactive, integrated threat assessment and response.

- Created investment prioritization framework for agricultural development

### 3. Analytics Infrastructure & Implementation (Months 6-12)

- Developed integrated analytics platform connecting production, import, and consumption data
- Created executive dashboards for ministerial decision-making
- Implemented monitoring framework tracking progress against strategic goals

## VALUE REALIZED

# Case Study: Ministry of Municipalities & Rural Affairs - Performance Intelligence & Organizational Design

**Industry:** Government / Municipal Services  
**Client Type:** National Government Agency

## CHALLENGE

Saudi Arabia's Ministry of Municipalities and Rural Affairs struggled to manage performance across hundreds of municipalities nationwide:

- No standardized approach to measuring municipal service delivery
- Fragmented IT systems with inconsistent data quality
- Organizational structure unclear on roles and accountability
- Leadership lacked visibility into which municipalities were performing and why

**The execution infrastructure problem:** Each municipality operated independently with different systems, processes, and standards—making national-level performance management impossible.

## OUR APPROACH

### 1. Organizational Design & Operating Model (Months 1-4)

- Conducted comprehensive assessment of ministry structure and municipality operations
- Redesigned organizational model clarifying roles, responsibilities, and reporting lines
- Established performance management framework spanning all municipalities
- Created governance structure connecting ministry leadership to municipal operations

### 2. Performance Analytics Platform Development (Months 4-9)

- Built Tableau-based dashboards tracking municipal performance nationwide
- Developed KPI framework spanning service delivery, financial management, and citizen satisfaction
- Implemented data integration layer connecting disparate municipal systems
- Created comparative analytics enabling ministry to identify best practices and underperformers

### 3. Capability Building & Change Management (Months 9-12)

- Trained ministry staff and municipal leaders on performance management framework
- Established regular performance review cadences at ministry and regional levels
- Implemented continuous improvement processes based on data insights
- Created knowledge-sharing mechanisms for high-performing municipalities

## VALUE REALIZED

### Organizational Transformation

- **Unified operating model** replacing fragmented, inconsistent municipal management
- **Performance visibility** enabling data-driven resource allocation across hundreds of municipalities
- **Best practice sharing** accelerating improvement in underperforming regions
- **Enhanced accountability** through transparent, standardized performance metrics

### Service Delivery Excellence

- Measurable improvements in municipal service delivery tracked through standardized KPIs
- Faster identification and resolution of performance issues
- Optimized resource allocation based on comparative performance data
- Foundation for smart city initiatives and AI-powered municipal services

### Timeline

- 12 months from organizational design to full platform deployment
- System continues as core performance management infrastructure for ministry

## KEY SUCCESS FACTORS

Unlike traditional government consulting that stops at organizational charts:

- Built production analytics infrastructure enabling ongoing performance management
- Embedded change management ensuring adoption across hundreds of municipalities
- Created sustainable governance model for continuous improvement
- Positioned ministry for digital transformation and smart city evolution

**Government Modernization Impact:**

This engagement transformed how Saudi Arabia manages municipal services nationwide—establishing data-driven performance management as the foundation for next-generation government service delivery.
